# ZELORI wedding invitation storefront

Open `index.html` to browse the storefront. Each portfolio card opens its matching invitation demo from **View Design → Open Invitation**; **Order This Design** keeps the selected design and moves to the request form.

## Included invitation demos

- Classic Ivory — `invitation-demo.html`
- Noir — `noir-invitation.html`
- Rosé — `rose-invitation.html`
- Minimal — `minimal-invitation.html`
- Royal — `royal-invitation.html`
- Editorial — `editorial-invitation.html`

Classic Ivory has its own `invitation-demo.css` and `invitation-demo.js`. The five themed pages share `invitation-templates.css` and `invitation-templates.js`; the storefront remains on `style.css` and `script.js`.

## Local preview

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. A static web server gives the most reliable local preview. The invitation RSVP forms and music are demonstrations; RSVP submissions are not stored. The storefront request form submits to the existing Formspree endpoint. On a successful submission, it displays the generated order reference and WhatsApp follow-up link.

## Order tracking

This archive does not include an order-tracking dashboard. Manage requests in Formspree and continue follow-up manually on WhatsApp until a separate, reviewed workflow is established.

The Digital Cards page presents twelve distinct, non-downloadable previews. Customers choose a style and send an order request; the finished personalized image is delivered after purchase.

## Preparing a public upload

- Upload the contents of this `ZELORI` folder to the static host's site root, preserving the HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and `assets` folder together. The site uses ordinary linked pages; no single-page-app rewrite rule is required.
- No order-tracking dashboard is included in this archive.
- Before launch, replace demo names, event dates, venues, map searches, and sample photos with approved customer details. RSVP responses are demo-only and are not stored.
- Confirm the live domain's Formspree submission and the WhatsApp contact link after upload.

## Order flow

The form collects the selected design and package, event details, and customer WhatsApp number. Current listed prices are 99 EGP for a Digital Card, 299 EGP for a Web Invitation, and 499 EGP for Custom Design (bespoke visual direction plus matching invitation page). RSVP response storage is not included. After Formspree accepts a request, contact the customer on WhatsApp to confirm scope, price and manual payment method/timing. The storefront does not collect online payments. Business WhatsApp: `+20 127 475 5728`.

Payment is discussed and handled manually on WhatsApp, with InstaPay offered only if agreed: the storefront does not collect payment or automatically reconcile transfers. A submission creates an order reference in the form request; it does not mean the order or payment is confirmed.
