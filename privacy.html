Privacy Notice

When you send an invitation request, ZELORI receives the names, wedding date and time, venue, selected design and package, notes, and phone/WhatsApp number that you enter. Please provide only information needed to discuss your order.

How we use your information

We use these details to review your request, contact you about its scope and price, and coordinate the invitation. The request form is submitted to Formspree, which stores submissions in the account inbox and may send notifications. Formspree says its systems are hosted in the United States, so your form data may be processed there. Read the Formspree Privacy Policy.

Sharing and retention

ZELORI uses the details to handle the inquiry and does not use this form for marketing subscriptions. Formspree's submission-history retention depends on the account plan: its documentation says the Free plan keeps 30 days of submission history and paid plans have longer retention. ZELORI has not verified the account's current plan or settings, so the exact retention period is not confirmed. See Formspree account limits.

To ask ZELORI to correct or delete information you submitted, message us on WhatsApp at +20 127 475 5728. We will review the request and handle deletion in the form account where available. Formspree may retain information as described in its policy.

Payment and RSVP

This website does not take online payments. Payment, including any InstaPay transfer, is discussed manually over WhatsApp after we review the request. RSVP forms shown on invitation previews are demonstrations; replies are not sent or saved.

Your choices

Do not include sensitive personal information in the order form. The privacy contact for this project is ZELORI via WhatsApp at +20 127 475 5728.
