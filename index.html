QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions.

What is the difference between a Digital Card and Web Invitation? +

A Digital Card is a personalized image you can share in a message. A Web Invitation is an interactive page with event details and an RSVP form demo. Replies are not sent or stored unless a separate RSVP service is connected.

How will I receive my invitation? +

After we confirm your order and payment details on WhatsApp, we deliver the finished Digital Card image or your Web Invitation link, depending on the option you chose.

Can I customize the names and date? +

Yes. Your names, wedding date, time, location and other agreed details can be personalized.

Can I add music and location? +

Web invitations can include agreed features such as music, location and countdown. RSVP forms shown in previews are demonstrations; replies are not sent or stored.

How do I order? +

Choose your favorite design, fill in the order form and we'll contact you through WhatsApp to complete the details.
